D-7, D-8 and D-9 investors who work at a Korean company (including invested companies) at full-time director level or higher, and E-1, E-3 to E-5 and E-7 professionals, can obtain permission for lecturing at a university within 90 days as short-term employment (C-4-5) activity outside their status. Submit the application form, passport and alien registration card, fee, the university president’s recommendation, a copy of the business registration, the employment contract (original and copy) and the original workplace head’s consent; regular lecturing beyond 90 days is a matter for headquarters approval.

## Key Summary

- Scope: lecturing at a university within 90 days, as short-term employment (C-4-5) activity outside status
- Eligible: investors (D-7, D-8, D-9) at full-time director level or higher, professionals (E-1, E-3 to E-5, E-7)
- Submit: application, passport and registration card, fee, president’s recommendation, business registration copy, employment contract, original workplace head’s consent
- Regular lecturing beyond 90 days requires headquarters approval

## 1. What activity is this?

A system that permits a foreign-invested company CEO or other outstanding professional who wants to lecture at a university within 90 days to do so as short-term employment (C-4-5) activity outside their status.

## 2. Who is covered?

Holders of investor-type statuses (D-7, D-8, D-9) who work at a Korean company (including invested companies) at full-time director level or higher, and holders of professional statuses (E-1, E-3 to E-5, E-7).

## 3. Which documents are submitted?

- Application form (Form No. 34), passport and alien registration card, fee
- Recommendation from the president (or dean)
- Copy of the business registration (or unique number certificate)
- Employment contract (original and copy)
- Consent of the head of the original workplace

## 4. What if it exceeds 90 days?

Anyone who wants to lecture regularly at a university beyond 90 days is a matter for headquarters approval. If you plan regular lectures, take this into account from the start.

## 5. What to check in advance

- Whether your position is full-time director level or higher, or you hold a covered professional status
- Whether the lecture period is within 90 days
- Whether you have obtained consent from the head of the original workplace

## 6. Common problems

- A lecture period that stretches past 90 days
- A university recommendation or employment contract in the wrong form
- Overlooking the original workplace’s consent

## Frequently Asked Questions

### May a D-8 representative lecture at a university?

Investors working at full-time director level or higher can obtain short-term employment (C-4-5) permission for lecturing within 90 days.

### Can professionals do it too?

Holders of E-1, E-3 to E-5 and E-7 are also covered.

### What recommendation is needed?

The recommendation of the university president (or dean).

### What if it exceeds 90 days?

Regular lecturing beyond 90 days is a matter for headquarters approval.

### Is consent from my current employer needed?

Yes, the consent of the head of the original workplace is among the required documents.
