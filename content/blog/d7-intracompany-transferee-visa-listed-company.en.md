## What is the D-7 Intracompany Transferee Visa?

The D-7 visa (Intracompany Transfer / 주재) allows foreign nationals employed by overseas subsidiaries or affiliates of Korean companies to work in Korea. This visa is particularly important for KOSPI/KOSDAQ listed companies that need to transfer key personnel from their overseas operations to Korean headquarters.

## Key Requirements

**Applicant Requirements:**
- Minimum **1 year of employment** at the overseas company before transfer
- Must hold a managerial, executive, or specialist position
- No criminal record in the past 5 years

**Sponsoring Company Requirements:**
- KOSPI/KOSDAQ listed company or foreign-invested company (FICI registered)
- Investment relationship (equity stake, joint venture) with the overseas entity
- No outstanding tax obligations or administrative sanctions

## Required Documents

**Applicant:**
- Valid passport (6+ months validity)
- Visa application form
- Employment certificate from overseas company (minimum 1 year)
- Transfer/assignment letter from Korean company
- Educational certificates

**Sponsoring Company:**
- Business registration certificate
- Corporate registry (issued within 3 months)
- KOSPI/KOSDAQ listing certificate
- Investment relationship proof (shareholder register, investment confirmation)
- Transfer plan and salary commitment letter

## Application Process

1. Document preparation (2–4 weeks)
2. Certificate of Visa Issuance (COE) application at Immigration Office (2–4 weeks)
3. Visa application at Korean consulate overseas (1–2 weeks)
4. Entry to Korea
5. Alien Registration Card application within 90 days

**Total Processing Time: 6–10 weeks**

## Family Accompanying

- Spouse: F-3 (Dependent) visa
- Minor children: F-1 (Family Visit) visa
- Employment for accompanying family members requires separate work visa
