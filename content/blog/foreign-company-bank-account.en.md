Opening a corporate bank account is one of the first tasks after incorporating a company in Korea. It seems straightforward, but in practice — especially when the CEO is a foreign national — the process often involves additional documentation or interviews.

---

## Required Documents

**Standard documents:**
- Corporate registry extract (issued within 3 months)
- Business registration certificate copy
- Articles of incorporation copy
- Shareholder register
- Corporate seal and seal certificate
- CEO's ID (passport or Alien Registration Card)

**Additional requirements for foreign CEOs:**
- Alien Registration Card or passport original
- Visa documentation (e.g., D-8 status)
- Employment certificate or related corporate contracts in some cases

> **Note:** Requirements vary by bank. Before visiting, confirm the exact document list with the bank's corporate banking representative.

---

## Process

1. **Select bank** — choose one with experience in foreign corporate accounts
2. **Make appointment** — corporate account opening often requires advance scheduling
3. **Prepare documents and visit**
4. **Interview with officer** — business purpose, expected transaction volume, etc.
5. **Account review** — typically 1–3 days
6. **Account opened** — online banking can be applied for simultaneously

---

## Common Rejection Reasons

| Reason | How to Address |
|--------|---------------|
| Insufficient identity verification for foreign CEO | Prepare additional ID documentation |
| Unclear business purpose | Prepare business plan or contracts |
| Unconfirmed visa status | Complete visa documentation |
| AML (anti-money laundering) review | Prepare source of funds documentation |
| Inability to verify physical office | Prepare office lease agreement |

## Choosing a Primary Bank

**Foreign exchange services:**
Companies with frequent overseas transfers (trading, IT outsourcing, etc.) should prioritize FX fee rates and processing speed. KEB Hana Bank has strong FX capabilities.

**Online banking:**
Banks offering English-language corporate internet banking are more convenient for foreign CEOs. Shinhan SOL Business and KEB Hana One Q Business are common options.

**Investment company loan products:**
Some banks offer dedicated financial products for foreign-invested companies, which may be advantageous long-term.

---

## Practical Tips

- **Apply for internet banking simultaneously:** Applying for corporate internet banking at the same time as account opening makes subsequent tax payments and payroll simpler.
- **Set up automatic transfers early:** Arrange automatic payments for taxes and social insurance premiums from the start.
- **Operate multiple accounts:** Keeping operating expenses and capital funds in separate accounts simplifies accounting.

---

## FAQ

**Q. Can someone other than the CEO open the account on behalf of the company?**
A. Some banks allow proxy account opening with a corporate seal certificate and proxy authorization. However, banks often require the CEO to appear in person for foreign-owned companies.

**Q. How long does account opening take?**
A. With complete documents, same-day or within 2–3 business days. Delays usually occur when the bank requests additional documentation.

**Q. Can we use a foreign bank such as Citibank or HSBC in Korea?**
A. Yes. However, they have fewer branches in Korea, which can make in-person transactions inconvenient. Tax payments and social insurance filings may be more complex.
