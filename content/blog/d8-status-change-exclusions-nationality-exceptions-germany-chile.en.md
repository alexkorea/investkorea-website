Changing to corporate investment (D-8) inside Korea from statuses that do not permit it is in principle not allowed; only with special grounds, such as recognized genuineness and an investment of 300 million won or more or substantial investment results, may the head of the competent immigration office allow it after close review. Irish, French and UK nationals who entered under D-3, E-9, E-10, H-2, G-1 or H-1 are listed as not allowed to change (nationals of other countries can), Germans who entered visa-free (B-1) may change to any long-term status except D-3, E-9 and H-1, and Chileans on a short-term visit (C-3-4) may change to D-7, D-8 or D-9 if the requirements are met.

- Principle: statuses not permitted to change to D-8 cannot change inside Korea; only special grounds (300 million won or more, etc.) are allowed after close review
- Listed as not allowed: Irish, French and UK nationals who entered under D-3, E-9, E-10, H-2, G-1 or H-1 (others can)

## 1. Can I change to D-8 inside Korea?

Changes to corporate investment (D-8) from statuses that do not permit it are in principle not allowed. In such cases you are advised to leave and enter with a visa.

## 2. Are there exceptions?

With special grounds such as recognized genuineness of the investment (for example an investment of 300 million won or more, or substantial investment results), the head of the competent regional immigration office may allow a change after close review.

## 3. What differs by nationality?

- Irish, French and UK nationals who entered under technical training (D-3), non-professional employment (E-9), seafarer (E-10), visiting employment (H-2), other (G-1) or working holiday (H-1) are listed as not allowed to change (nationals of other countries can).
- Germans who entered visa-free (B-1) can change to any long-term status except D-3, E-9 and H-1, with the period being the per-status maximum per grant.
- Chileans with a short-term visit status (C-3-4) can receive a change permit under the Korea-Chile FTA if they meet the requirements of intra-company transferee (D-7), corporate investment (D-8) or trade management (D-9).

## 4. What are the requirements for the Chile FTA change?

Business persons who intend to act as managers or executives or in capacities involving essential technology, mainly engaged in trade in goods or services, or who have invested or are in the process of investing substantial capital and provide advice or essential technical services for establishing, developing, managing or operating that investment. The documents to submit are those that can confirm the qualification.

## 5. What to check before applying

- Your current status and the type of visa at entry
- Whether a nationality exception (Germany, Chile) applies
- The investment amount and results (300 million won benchmark)

## 6. Common problems

- Filing without knowing the nationality and entry-status combination is not allowed
- Expecting a change inside Korea despite a small investment
- Not preparing enough documents to confirm qualifications for the Chile FTA route
