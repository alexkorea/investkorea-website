# D-8-4 Technology Startup Visa Korea 2026

The D-8-4 (Corporate Investment – Technology Startup) visa is issued to foreign nationals who wish to establish and operate a technology or knowledge-based startup in Korea.

## Key Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Business field | Technology/knowledge-based (IT, bio, manufacturing) |
| Education | Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience |
| Institutional recommendation | Required from designated agencies |
| Company | Korean corporation (established or in process) |

## Designated Recommending Organizations

- Korea Startup Agency (창업진흥원)
- TIPS Program operators
- Regional Technoparks
- Business Incubation Centers

## Required Documents

- Visa application form, passport
- Business plan (in Korean)
- Recommendation letter from designated agency
- Corporate registration certificate
- Diploma and career certificate

## Application Process

1. Consult with a designated startup agency and obtain recommendation
2. Establish Korean corporation
3. Apply for D-8-4 status at immigration office
4. Processing: approx. 2–4 weeks
5. Begin startup operations after visa issuance

## Stay Period

- Initial: 1 year
- Renewal: 1–2 years based on business performance
