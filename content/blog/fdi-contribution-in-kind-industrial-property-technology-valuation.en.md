Yes. The Foreign Investment Promotion Act calls what a foreign investor contributes to own shares a "contribution object," and besides foreign currency (means of external payment) it lists capital goods, industrial property rights and technology, certain shares, real estate in Korea, and residual assets when a Korean branch or office is closed and converted into a corporation. For in-kind contributions, the key question is who confirms the value and how, and the Act provides special rules for capital goods and industrial property rights that replace procedures under the Commercial Act.

This guide is for foreign investors who plan to contribute equipment or patents of an overseas headquarters to a Korean corporation, and for staff of Korean joint venture partners.

## Key Summary

- Contribution objects: means of external payment such as foreign currency, capital goods, proceeds from shares, industrial property rights, intellectual property rights and technology, residual assets on closing a branch or office or dissolving a corporation, repayment amounts of loans, certain shares, real estate in Korea, and proceeds from disposing of shares or real estate
- The intellectual property rights are copyrights used in industrial activities and semiconductor layout-design rights; the shares include shares of foreign corporations listed on a foreign stock market
- For in-kind contribution of capital goods, the completion certificate confirmed by the Commissioner of the Korea Customs Service is treated as the inspector's investigation report under the Commercial Act
- If a technology valuation agency values industrial property rights, the valuation is treated as an appraisal by an authorized appraiser under the Commercial Act
- Capital goods imported for contribution can be submitted for review and confirmation before acceptance of the import declaration, and confirmed goods are treated as having import approval
- After paying in the contribution object, the company must register as a foreign-invested company; registration based on a sham payment is cancelled

## 1. What can be contributed besides cash?

The contribution objects defined by the Foreign Investment Promotion Act are as follows.

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Means of external payment | Means of external payment under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act, or domestic means of payment obtained by exchanging them |
| Capital goods | Machinery, equipment, facility items, tools, parts and accessories as industrial facilities |
| Proceeds | Proceeds from shares acquired under the Act |
| Industrial property rights, etc. | Industrial property rights, intellectual property rights defined by Presidential Decree, equivalent technology and rights to use them |
| Residual assets | Remaining assets distributed on liquidation when a Korean branch or office is closed and converted into a domestic corporation, or when a domestic corporation is dissolved |
| Repayment amounts | Repayment amounts of long-term loans or other borrowings from abroad |
| Shares | Shares defined by Presidential Decree |
| Real estate | Real estate located in Korea |
| Disposal proceeds | Proceeds from disposing of shares in Korean companies and real estate owned by a foreigner |

Capital goods include, besides industrial facilities such as ships, vehicles and aircraft, livestock, seeds, trees and fish and shellfish needed for agriculture, forestry and fisheries, raw materials and spare parts recognized as needed for the first trial run, and the freight and insurance for their introduction as well as technology or services for installation or advice.

## 2. Which intellectual property rights and shares qualify?

The Enforcement Decree defines the contributable intellectual property rights as copyrights under the Copyright Act that are used in industrial activities, and layout-design rights for semiconductor integrated circuits. Industrial property rights such as patents, equivalent technology and rights to use them are listed directly in the Act as contribution objects.

The contributable shares are shares of foreign corporations listed on a foreign stock market, and shares owned by a foreigner under the Act or the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act. Shares of unlisted foreign corporations are not on this list, so they need to be confirmed case by case.

## 3. Who confirms the value when machinery or equipment is contributed in kind?

Under the Commercial Act, an in-kind contribution is in principle examined by an inspector. Under the Foreign Investment Promotion Act, when a foreign investor contributes capital goods in kind, the in-kind contribution completion certificate in which the Commissioner of the Korea Customs Service has confirmed the performance of the contribution and the type, quantity and price of the objects is treated as the inspector's investigation report under the Commercial Act. The same applies when capital goods are contributed after the company is established.

To obtain the completion confirmation, two copies of the application on the prescribed form (Form No. 25) are submitted to the Commissioner of the Korea Customs Service. The Commissioner checks the import declaration certificate through shared administrative information, and if the applicant does not consent to this check, the applicant must attach the document. After confirmation, the Commissioner notifies the Governor of the Bank of Korea.

## 4. What should be done before importing the equipment? Review and confirmation of imported goods

Capital goods imported by a foreign investor as a contribution object can be reviewed and confirmed by the competent minister, and confirmed capital goods are treated as having import approval under the Foreign Trade Act.

To receive review and confirmation, an import goods specification stating the quantity, specifications, price and manufacturer of the capital goods must be prepared and submitted before acceptance of the import declaration. This work is entrusted to the heads of foreign exchange banks and the head of the Korea Trade-Investment Promotion Agency, and three copies of the application (Form No. 24) are submitted with documents proving the price, such as a sales confirmation.

| Situation | Application deadline |
|---|---|
| General rule | Before acceptance of the import declaration |
| When customs duty is paid under the Customs Act | Within 5 days of receiving the payment notice |
| When the goods have not left the bonded area | Within 15 days of the date the import declaration is accepted |

## 5. How are patents and technology valued for contribution?

If a technology valuation agency defined by Presidential Decree values the industrial property rights, the valuation is treated as an appraisal by an authorized appraiser under the Commercial Act. The valuation agencies here are those defined in the enforcement decree of the venture business law. Which agencies qualify and how long a valuation takes need to be confirmed case by case.

When shares, bonds or real estate in Korea are contributed, a copy of the inspector's investigation report or the appraiser's appraisal result under the Commercial Act must be submitted when registering the foreign-invested company.

## 6. How is the foreign-invested company registered after the contribution?

When the payment of the contribution object is completed, the foreign investor or foreign-invested company must register as a foreign-invested company. Depending on the contribution method, copies of the following documents are attached to the registration application.

- Other than in-kind contributions: a foreign currency purchase certificate or foreign currency deposit certificate identifying the remitter
- Contribution of capital goods: the in-kind contribution completion certificate
- Contribution of shares, bonds or real estate in Korea: the inspector's investigation report or the appraiser's appraisal result
- Common: the corporate registration certificate, the shareholder register, and the business registration certificate or unique number certificate

If a company registers as a foreign-invested company by faking the payment of the contribution object, the registration is cancelled. Inflating the price or pretending to contribute goods that were never actually transferred can undo the registration itself.

## 7. Common problems

- Clearing the equipment through customs first and then missing the deadline for review and confirmation of imported goods
- Planning the schedule around the Commercial Act inspector procedure without the in-kind contribution completion confirmation
- Assuming shares of an unlisted foreign corporation can be used as a contribution object
- Not checking whether the valuation agency for a technology contribution is one defined in the Enforcement Decree
- A Korean joint venture partner negotiating on book value without knowing the special revaluation rule for contribution objects (the first day of each month as the revaluation date)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can used machinery from the overseas headquarters be contributed?

Capital goods such as machinery and equipment as industrial facilities are contribution objects. The completion certificate in which the customs authority confirmed type, quantity and price replaces the inspector's investigation report under the Commercial Act.

### Who appraises a patent that is contributed?

If a technology valuation agency defined in the Enforcement Decree values the industrial property rights, the valuation is treated as an appraisal by an authorized appraiser under the Commercial Act.

### Can a software copyright be contributed?

Copyrights under the Copyright Act that are used in industrial activities are included in the intellectual property rights that can be contributed. Whether a specific right qualifies needs to be confirmed case by case.

### Can branch assets be contributed when converting a Korean branch into a corporation?

When a foreigner closes a Korean branch or office and converts it into another domestic corporation, the remaining assets distributed to that foreigner on liquidation are contribution objects.

### By when must review and confirmation of imported equipment be requested?

In principle, before acceptance of the import declaration. If customs duty is paid, within 5 days of the payment notice; if the goods have not left the bonded area, within 15 days of acceptance of the import declaration.
