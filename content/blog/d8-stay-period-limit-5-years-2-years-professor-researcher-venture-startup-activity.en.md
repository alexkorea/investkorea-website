The maximum stay per grant under Corporate Investment (D-8) is 5 years for investing in a corporation (D-8-1) and investing in a sole proprietorship (D-8-3), and 2 years for venture investment (D-8-2) and tech startup (D-8-4). Registered foreigners with Professor (E-1), Research (E-3), Technology Instruction (E-4) or Professional (E-5) status who meet the D-8-2 or D-8-4(S) requirements may engage only in activity related to their own research or field, as activity outside their status.

## Key Summary

- Maximum stay per grant: 5 years for D-8-1 and D-8-3, 2 years for D-8-2 and D-8-4
- Outside-status activity covers registered foreigners meeting D-8-1/D-8-3 requirements and E-1, E-3, E-4, E-5 holders meeting D-8-2/D-8-4(S) requirements
- E-1, E-3, E-4, E-5 holders: only D-8-2/D-8-4(S) activity related to their own research or field
- Documents follow the visa issuance and status change standards; points-system applicants are exempt from education proof

## 1. How long is D-8 granted?

The maximum stay that can be granted at once is 5 years for D-8-1 and D-8-3 and 2 years for D-8-2 and D-8-4. The actual period is set during review.

## 2. Who may carry out activity outside status?

Registered foreigners meeting the D-8-1 and D-8-3 requirements, and registered foreigners with Professor (E-1), Research (E-3), Technology Instruction (E-4) or Professional (E-5) status who meet the D-8-2 and D-8-4(S) requirements.

## 3. What is the scope for E-1, E-3, E-4 and E-5 holders?

Only D-8-2 and D-8-4(S) activity related to your own research or field is allowed. Startup activity in an unrelated field is outside this scope.

## 4. Which documents are submitted?

For each of D-8-1, D-8-2, D-8-3 and D-8-4, the document standards for visa issuance and status change apply. Points-system applicants are exempt from education-requirement proof.

## 5. Points to check before filing

- Whether your current status (E-1, E-3, E-4, E-5, etc.) matches the field of the startup activity
- Planning around the 2-year limit for D-8-2 and D-8-4
- Whether you fall under the points system or an exemption

## 6. Common problems

- Starting a business unrelated to your research field and exceeding the activity scope
- Mixing up the 5-year and 2-year limits
- Over-preparing education proof when exempt

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the maximum for D-8-1?

The maximum stay per grant is 5 years.

### And for D-8-2 and D-8-4?

The maximum stay per grant is 2 years.

### Can an E-3 researcher pursue startup activity?

Only if the D-8-2 or D-8-4(S) requirements are met and the activity relates to your own research or field.

### Which status holders are covered?

Registered foreigners with Professor (E-1), Research (E-3), Technology Instruction (E-4) or Professional (E-5) status.

### Must I submit education proof?

Points-system applicants are exempt from education-requirement proof.
