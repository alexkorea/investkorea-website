For D-8-1 the investment target must be a Korean corporation, and you must meet one of two routes: an investment of 100 million won or more while owning 10% or more of the voting shares, or owning shares while concluding a director dispatch or appointment contract. Only completed (established) corporations count, and persons hired in Korea are excluded from essential personnel.

## Key Summary

- Target: a Korean corporation (completed corporations only)
- Route 1: investment of 100 million won or more plus 10% or more of voting shares
- Route 2: owning shares while concluding a director dispatch or appointment contract
- Essential personnel exclude persons hired in Korea

## 1. What kind of status is D-8-1?

It is one of the Corporate Investment (D-8) statuses for essential personnel engaged in management, administration, production or technology at a Korean corporation that is a foreign-invested company under the Foreign Investment Promotion Act.

## 2. Which corporation can be the target?

The target must be a Korean corporation, and only completed (established) corporations count. This is hard to meet before the corporation is set up.

## 3. Route 1: the 10% stake?

Investment of 100 million won or more and ownership of 10% or more of the invested corporation’s voting shares (cited to Article 2(2) item 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Foreign Investment Promotion Act).

## 4. Route 2: director dispatch or appointment contract?

Owning shares while concluding a director dispatch or appointment contract (cited to item 2 of the same provision). It shows a management relationship by contract instead of a share percentage.

## 5. Who is not essential personnel?

The manual states that persons hired in Korea are excluded. Locally hired staff unconnected to the investor should consider other statuses such as E-7.

## 6. Common problems

- A stake below 10% with no director dispatch contract documents
- Investment of 100 million won or more but the corporation not yet established
- Shareholder register and corporate registry entries that do not match

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is D-8-1 impossible below a 10% stake?

The manual also lists owning shares while concluding a director dispatch or appointment contract as a route.

### What investment amount is needed?

Under route 1 the investment must be 100 million won or more.

### Can I invest in a corporation not yet established?

D-8-1 covers completed (established) corporations only.

### Does a locally hired employee qualify for D-8-1?

Persons hired in Korea are excluded from essential personnel.

### Must I satisfy both routes?

The manual presents them as alternatives; which route is met is judged on the structure.
