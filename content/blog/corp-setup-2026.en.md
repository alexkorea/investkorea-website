# How to Set Up a Company in Korea as a Foreigner 2026

Foreign investors establishing a Korean corporation are subject to the Foreign Investment Promotion Act and the Commercial Act.

## Entity Types

- **Corporation (Jusik Hoesa)**: 1+ shareholders, no minimum capital required by law (1 million KRW in practice)
- **Limited Company (Yuhan Hoesa)**: Suitable for small foreign subsidiaries
- **Branch Office**: Extension of the parent company, no separate legal entity

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **FDI Notification** — File with Korea Eximbank or KOTRA before remitting funds
2. **Capital Remittance** — Transfer investment capital via a Korean foreign exchange bank
3. **Corporate Registration** — Register company name, purpose, capital, and representative director
4. **Business Registration** — Register with the regional tax office
5. **FDI Enterprise Registration** — Register as a foreign-invested enterprise with KOTRA

## Common Bottlenecks

The FDI notification must precede the capital remittance. Reversing the order triggers fines under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act. If the representative director is a foreigner, a valid Korean stay permit is required.
