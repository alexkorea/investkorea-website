The documents proving D-8 investment fund inflow depend on the form of contribution. For a cash contribution, submit the foreign currency export permit (report) from the customs or a bank of the home country (where applicable) and an investment fund inflow statement (remittance confirmation, foreign exchange purchase certificate, customs declaration, etc.); for an in-kind contribution, submit a copy of the in-kind contribution completion confirmation issued by the Commissioner of the Korea Customs Service and a copy of the customs import declaration certificate.

## Key Summary

- Cash: foreign currency export permit (report), where applicable, plus an inflow statement (remittance confirmation, foreign exchange purchase certificate, customs declaration, etc.)
- In-kind: completion confirmation copy (Customs Service commissioner) plus customs import declaration certificate copy
- Submitted with: business registration copy, corporate registry certificate, original shareholder change statement, investment enterprise registration copy
- Those who obtained D-8 directly at a Korean mission follow the status change document standards

## 1. Where do the fund inflow documents fit?

“Documents proving investment fund inflow” are among the basic documents for status changes such as D-8-1, and the same item appears in extension documents.

## 2. What do I submit for a cash contribution?

The foreign currency export permit (report) from the customs or a bank of the home country (where applicable) and an investment fund inflow statement. The statement can rely on remittance confirmations, foreign exchange purchase certificates, customs declarations and the like.

## 3. What do I submit for an in-kind contribution?

A copy of the in-kind contribution completion confirmation (issued by the Commissioner of the Korea Customs Service) and a copy of the customs import declaration certificate.

## 4. What else is submitted with them?

The basic documents also include a business registration copy, corporate registry certificate, the original shareholder change statement and an investment enterprise registration copy. For dispatched work, the dispatch order and certificate of employment are included.

## 5. What if I entered with a D-8 visa obtained at a Korean mission?

A foreigner who obtained D-8 status directly at a Korean mission submits the documents standard for a status change application (excluding those who obtained the visa through a visa issuance certificate).

## 6. Common problems

- Remittance records and the inflow statement not matching in amount
- An in-kind contribution without customs documents
- Not checking whether the foreign currency export permit applies

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which documents are needed for a cash contribution?

The foreign currency export permit (report), where applicable, and an inflow statement (remittance confirmation, foreign exchange purchase certificate, customs declaration, etc.).

### And for an in-kind contribution?

A copy of the completion confirmation (Customs Service commissioner) and a copy of the customs import declaration certificate.

### Does everyone need a foreign currency export permit?

The manual marks it as “where applicable,” so those to whom it applies submit it.

### Is the shareholder change statement needed?

Yes, the original is among the basic documents.

### Are the same documents needed at extension?

The extension documents also list an item for proof of investment fund inflow.
