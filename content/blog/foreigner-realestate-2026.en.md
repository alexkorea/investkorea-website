# How Foreigners Can Buy Property in Korea 2026

Foreign nationals purchasing real estate in Korea are subject to both the Real Estate Transaction Report Act and the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act.

## Required Notifications

1. **Real Estate Transaction Report** — Must be filed within 30 days of signing the contract with the local district office
2. **Foreign Exchange Report** — Required for transactions of 100 million KRW or more, filed with Bank of Korea or a foreign exchange bank
3. **Land Acquisition Permit** — Prior approval needed for designated zones (military protection areas, etc.)

## Taxes

- **Acquisition Tax**: 1–12% for residential (based on purchase price), 4% for commercial
- **Capital Gains Tax**: Varies by holding period and number of properties
- **Comprehensive Real Estate Tax**: Applies above a threshold based on official price

## Common Bottleneck

Proof of fund origin has become stricter. When remitting from overseas, retain all foreign exchange transaction records.
