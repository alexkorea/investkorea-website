## Opening a Bank Account in Korea as a Foreigner 2026

Requirements differ depending on your residency status and purpose.

## Personal Account (Foreign Residents)
| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Passport | Original |
| Alien Registration Card | Issued by immigration |
| Proof of address | Lease agreement, utility bill |

Short-term visitors (tourist/business) may only access restricted accounts.

## Corporate Account (Foreign-Invested Company)
Bring: business registration certificate + corporate registry + foreign investment declaration. A foreign exchange account is required for international transfers.

## Recommended Banks
- **KEB Hana / Woori Bank**: Strong multilingual service
- **IBK Enterprise Bank**: Specialist in foreign-invested SME accounts
- **Shinhan / Kookmin**: Best mobile app experience

Always call ahead — document requirements vary by branch.
